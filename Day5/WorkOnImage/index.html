Gaussian Blur

cv2.GaussianBlur

Applies a bell-curve Gaussian kernel. Smooths details smoothly. Used for reducing image noise & background blurring.

Median Blur

cv2.medianBlur

Takes the median pixel of neighboring window. Outliers (salt-and-pepper noise) are replaced without eroding sharp edges.

Sharpening

cv2.filter2D

Subtracts low-frequencies using Laplacian kernel [[0,-1,0],[-1,5,-1],[0,-1,0]] to boost edge transitions.
